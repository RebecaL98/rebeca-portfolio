Hi-Top Sneakers

Overview

Hi-Top Sneakers is a college project where I explored moving a boutique shoe shop from the high street to an online store. I designed the frontend to have a premium feel for sneaker collectors and built a secure PHP and MySQL admin panel behind the scenes so staff can easily manage products and stock levels.

Role & Tech Stack

  • E-Commerce UI/UX Design
  • Front-end Engineering (HTML/CSS/JS)
  • Back-end Development (PHP/MySQL)
  • Secure Admin Dashboard
